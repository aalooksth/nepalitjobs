## 1. Overview
Nepal IT Jobs is a free, open-access platform built to bridge the discovery gap between software engineers, QA professionals, designers, product managers, and technology companies operating in Nepal.

---

## 2. Searching & Filtering Jobs
- **Keyword Search**: Enter any skill, technology, or title (e.g., `React`, `Python`, `QA`, `Flutter`, `Senior`) into the top search bar.
- **Seniority Filters**: Filter by `Intern`, `Junior`, `Mid`, `Senior`, or `Lead`.
- **Domain & Industry**: Choose specific fields such as HealthIT, Fintech, Media Tech, or Custom Software.
- **Experience Range**: Filter by years of experience required.

---

## 3. Exploring Company Profiles
Navigate to the **Companies Directory** (`/companies.html`):
- Click any company card to open its detailed drawer.
- View official tech stacks, client types, office locations, year founded, and employee count.
- Direct links provide quick access to:
  - **🌐 Website**: Company homepage.
  - **💼 Careers**: Direct company jobs board.
  - **👔 LinkedIn**: Official LinkedIn organization page.

---

## 4. Using the Interactive Map
- The interactive map displays all company headquarters and engineering offices using customized company logo markers.
- Click any marker to view the company name, location, number of open vacancies, and a link to open its profile.
- Filter companies by location (Kathmandu Valley, Pokhara, Biratnagar, Butwal, Chitwan) to inspect regional tech hubs.

---

## 5. Comparing Companies
- Check the compare box on any 2 to 4 company cards.
- Click **Compare (N)** in the floating action bar.
- A side-by-side comparison modal will evaluate tech stacks, company sizes, founded years, headquarters, and active open roles.

---

## 6. Applying for Vacancies
Each job card provides:
- Direct link to the job posting.
- Instructions on whether to apply through an ATS (Workable, BambooHR, Recruitee), direct email, or LinkedIn.
- Key skills and requirements tagged for quick scanning.
