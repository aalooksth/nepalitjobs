Geographic Distribution of Tech Employers

Kathmandu Valley serves as the primary capital for IT services with key clusters in Lalitpur (Sanepa, Bakhundole, Pulchowk, Jawalakhel) and Kathmandu (Naxal, Durbarmarg, Thapathali, Panipokhari). Regional tech hubs are emerging in:

  • Pokhara: Embedded systems, hardware, and IoT innovation.
  • Biratnagar: Field sales automation, supply chain software, and ERP.
  • Butwal: Hotel and restaurant POS engines, commercial billing systems.
  • Chitwan: Full-stack offshore engineering and cloud consultancy.
💼

Product vs Services Firms

Nepal's ecosystem features a balanced mix of global outsourcing partners and native fintech/product organizations:

  • FinTech & Domestic Scale: F1Soft Group (eSewa), Khalti by IME, Hamro Patro, InfoDevelopers.
  • Global Delivery & Enterprise Healthcare: Leapfrog Technology, Cotiviti / Infinite, Verisk, Cedar Gate Technologies, Maitri Services, Docsumo.
  • AI & Data Engineering: Fusemachines, Guardsix (Logpoint), CloudFactory, GrowByData.

Frequently Asked Questions: Tech Companies in Nepal

How are companies verified and added to Nepal IT Jobs?

Every company listed has a physical office and verified engineering presence in Nepal, complete with coordinates, tech stack evaluations, career portal links, and automated ATS vacancy aggregation.

Can I compare two companies side-by-side?

Yes. Select up to 3 companies using the checkbox or comparison button on any company card, then click "Compare" to view side-by-side breakdowns of founding year, company size, tech stack, and active vacancies.
